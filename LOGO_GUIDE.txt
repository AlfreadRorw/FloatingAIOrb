ALF DOWNLOADER LOGO GUIDE

Main app logo
- PNG 1024x1024
- Transparent background
- Monochrome black/white
- Keep the main mark inside the central 72% safe area

Adaptive foreground
- PNG 432x432
- Transparent background
- Keep important details inside roughly 66% center safe area

Dock icons if you replace the built-in Material icons
- 96x96 PNG
- Transparent background
- Download: downward arrow / tray
- History: circular arrow / clock-like history mark
- Settings: simple gear

Recommended visual direction
- Pure black #000000
- Pure white #FFFFFF
- Optional gray #9E9E9E
- No gradients
- No emoji
